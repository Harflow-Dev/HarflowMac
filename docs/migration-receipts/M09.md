## Stage-1 fix: Recent repository chips (2026-11-19)

Reported from the owner's stage-1 look at Harflow Check `24f247b7`. In the new-task composer, the repository
popover's Recent chips were squeezed until their names wrapped one or two characters per line. Recent also
still listed a worktree that had been deleted.

Cause and fix:

- **Squeezed chips.** The chips sat in a fixed-width `HStack`, so each name was compressed and wrapped.
  - A windowless sizing reproduction at the popover's 328 pt content width measured 216 pt for five long
    names, against at most 130 pt for whole one-line chips. A single long name wrapped to three lines.
  - The row now uses the existing `PillFlowLayout`, moved to its own file with a pure, tested `rows` helper
    and a row alignment option. ToolGroupCard's placement is unchanged.
  - Each chip is one line, truncates at the tail and is at most 240 pt (`HfRadius.Metrics.recentRepoChipMaxWidth`).
    Whole chips wrap to new rows.
  - The full path stays in the tooltip and the accessibility hint.
- **Deleted folders.** Recents were derived from the task listing with no existence check.
  - They are now filtered off the main actor to existing absolute directories
    (`NewSessionUseCase.existingDirectories(among:)`), checked in batches of 20 until five exist. One
    cancellable task runs behind a generation fence.
  - They refresh when the composer appears and each time the repository popover opens, so a folder deleted
    while the composer stays open drops out on the next open.
  - Recents are empty until the first check completes, so an unchecked path never shows.
  - Missing folders are dropped, not marked: there is no drawn treatment for them, and a missing folder can't
    be chosen anyway.

Design: HarflowDesign `7de89a4` draws no open repository picker; only the closed repository pill appears in
`new-task/centered-*`. The chips follow the nearest drawn rule, `.hd-pill` / `.hd-pill__label` in
`v2/styles/bundle.css:118-122` (no wrapping, at most 240 px, tail ellipsis). That rule describes the closed
pill, so applying it to the Recent chips is provisional.

Owner item: the repository picker's open state, including the Recent chips and their missing-folder behaviour,
is undrawn.

## Approval rule at the minimum window (2026-11-19)

Resolves the owner question in "Hosted fixes" above. The owner approved the rule on 2026-11-19:

- While a caller approval is pending in the selected pane, the approval card gets its full natural height first.
- The feed gives up space first, below its 80 pt floor, down to the step-count line.
- If the card still does not fit, it scrolls internally and its Approve/Reject actions stay pinned and visible.
- Once the approval resolves, the 80 pt floor returns.
- Nothing changes when no approval is pending.

Implementation:

- **Demand.** `CallerApprovalDemandKey` is a max-reduce preference read by the pane.
  - It reports the natural height of the visually pending cards only, plus padding, spacing and the decision bars.
  - Visually pending covers reserved, sending and unknown-disposition requests, and a blocked card.
  - Settled history and "Load more" scroll below the pending cards inside the region.
- **Sizing** (`WorkflowSelectedPaneSizing`):
  - the pending top region is `max(0, min(existingTop, pane − reserved − demand))`;
  - the pending approval height is `min(natural, column − chrome)`;
  - the idle rule (45% cap, 80 pt feed floor) is unchanged.
- **Pinned layout.** In the selected pane, `CallerApprovalPanel(pinsDecisionControls:)`:
  - lays out a card `ScrollView`, with awaiting cards first and a stable order within each group;
  - places one decision bar per pending card with controls outside that scroll view;
  - labels each bar "Tool · command", truncated in the middle, with the full text in `.help` and the accessibility label.
  - **Several bars that exceed the region:** they scroll as a stack. The card keeps a viewport of `min(pending natural height, 80 pt)`, and the stack never shrinks below its tallest bar. A single bar is never scrolled.
